My Archive – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

My Archive does not collect, store, send or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. No servers. No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • The fics, covers, ratings and settings you add are stored only on your device, encrypted with your password (if you set one).
  • Files you choose or share to the app are read on your device and never uploaded anywhere.
  • Backup files are created only when you ask, and saved where you choose.
  • Links inside fics (for example to a fic's original page) open in your own browser, outside the app.

Because nothing leaves your device, the developer cannot see, recover or delete your data. Deleting the app deletes your archive, so keep a backup file.

My Archive is an independent reader app. It is not affiliated with Archive of Our Own, the Organization for Transformative Works, or any website.
